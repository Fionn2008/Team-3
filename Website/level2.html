ABOUT THE GAME

Level 2 takes place in a forest where Spuder-Man encounters the Green Goblin after the portal from New York to Dublin has been hacked, which has led to villians entering Ireland.

Play as Spider-Man and attack Green Goblin to advance to Level 3.Collect the collectables on the way such as ther chicken fillet roll and spuds which will replenish the health bar.
