The Spectacular Spuderman

Users play as Paddy Walsh who was bitten by a radioactive spider. The Irish Spider-Man ends up in cage where he is challenged by the almight Connor Mc Gregor. After the fight Paddy discovers that the portal from Dublin To New York has been tampered with. This has resulted in villians from the spider-man multiverse entering Ireland. In the second level Spider-Man comes across The Green Goblin in a forest. Spider-Man has to use his strenght,stamina and web shooter to defeat this enemy. In level three, Spider-Man travels across the sea and ends up in New York. The final battle takes place on the Brooklyn bridge where Spider-Man has to face Doc Ock.
